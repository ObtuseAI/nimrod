# ADR-072: Place intelligence research beneath constitutional authority

Status: accepted for replay implementation  
Date: 2026-07-15

## Context

The owner proposed an engine whose subject is intelligence itself: discovery, research, creativity, problem solving, scientific testing, recursive improvement, and theory construction. Implemented as another autonomous sovereign agent, this would duplicate authority, invite self-verification, and allow method optimization to become uncontrolled power expansion.

CACIS W3 already requires competing hypotheses, adversarial challenge, metacognition, and settlement. That wave is the correct first bounded application for a reusable research engine.

## Decision

Create the Constitutional Intelligence Research Engine as a cross-domain nimrod package and use it to implement CACIS W3.

Every experiment must preregister null, candidate, rival, and unknown hypotheses; bind immutable source evidence; define predictions and hard failures before execution; preserve material challenges; use a read-only verifier role; retain metacognitive uncertainty; and emit at most a candidate-only theory with explicit scope and falsifiers.

The Constitutional Kernel remains the only authority plane. CIRE cannot authorize, execute, change policy, access credentials, contact targets, verify itself, promote candidates, modify the active baseline, or modify the constitution.

The initial W3 result is a deterministic two-case method-structure replay. Logical verifier separation is accepted as replay evidence only. Separate-process, separate-account, external, hidden, forward, and production verification remain unproven.

## Consequences

- Intelligence research becomes reusable across domains without making security assumptions universal.
- CACIS gains a concrete Hypothesis Cortex rather than a prose-only agent swarm.
- Successful prompts are not promoted as laws; scoped, falsifiable theories become candidate evidence.
- Null, rival, unknown, and counter-evidence survive even when the primary predictions pass.
- W4 must meter research cost before recurring or idle-cycle research can be enabled.
- W5 remains the only path from candidate theory toward governed genome evaluation, and it cannot self-promote.

## Rejected alternatives

- A permanent self-improving super-agent with ambient tools or credentials.
- Scalar answer-quality optimization without preregistration or competing hypotheses.
- Automatic promotion after replay success.
- Claims of independent or general intelligence improvement from same-process structural fixtures.
