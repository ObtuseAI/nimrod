# Edge design-partner evidence kit

## Purpose

The first design-partner program evaluates whether people can correctly understand Edge evidence and authority boundaries before the product gains endpoint authority. It is not a sales beta, deployment authorization, production-protection claim, or permission to contact external people.

## Cohort and recruitment gate

The target is five partners and the maximum is eight across security-conscious individuals, small technical teams, and Windows operators. Named partners have not been selected, recruitment has not started, and no participant has been contacted. Counsel/privacy review and explicit participant consent are required before any session.

## Evaluation tasks

Each consented participant must be able to:

1. distinguish live evidence from replayed evidence before interpreting a verdict;
2. explain the replayed process, destination, deterministic policy match, and confidence without claiming containment;
3. locate missing publisher, parent, destination, and path-classification evidence;
4. distinguish a Budget 1 proposal from authorization, execution, and verified outcome;
5. export local evidence using an operator-chosen alias without raw telemetry or credentials.

## Measurement gate

The planned floor is 80% task completion, 80% evidence comprehension, 80% privacy acceptance, and zero critical misunderstandings about origin, authority, execution, or recovery. Averages cannot erase a critical misunderstanding. Every failure and dissent record remains evidence.

The exit gate additionally requires completed consented sessions, task metrics, a misunderstanding log, privacy feedback, and residual-risk review. Meeting the research gate does not authorize production claims or endpoint action.

## Data boundary

- no raw endpoint telemetry collection;
- no external telemetry upload;
- no credential collection;
- no background surveillance;
- no screen recording by default;
- operator-chosen aliases only;
- local evidence export demonstrated with repository fixtures unless a later consented protocol explicitly permits more.

## Session script

1. Explain the study purpose, data boundary, withdrawal path, and no-protection status.
2. Confirm written consent and the operator-selected alias.
3. Present the Edge replay view without coaching.
4. Run the five tasks and capture only structured task outcomes and participant-provided notes.
5. Ask the participant to identify misleading, alarming, or ambiguous language.
6. Review what was and was not collected, offer local evidence deletion/export, and record withdrawal or retention choice.
7. Preserve results as research evidence; do not convert them into marketing claims.
