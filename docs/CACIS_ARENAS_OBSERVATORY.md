# CACIS W6 arenas and Observatory

W6 evaluates fifteen explicitly synthetic deterministic replay scenarios across fourteen dimensions: detection quality, error rates, calibration, recovery, verification, containment, investigation and resource efficiency, agent contribution, genome and evolution quality, metacognition, and governance preservation.

Every arena binds a fixture scenario and expected evidence list. The metrics exercise evaluation and display semantics; they are synthetic test values, not observed product performance. All fifteen arenas remain blocked from live operation. No arena targets a public host or performs execution, and no aggregate can suppress a hard failure.

The Observatory projection is freshness-bound, threshold-signed by two distinct simulated governance roles, and display-only. It binds the W5 genome and W6 arena digests, preserves partial knowledge and missing evidence, and has no authorization, execution, dissent-suppression, policy-change, or promotion capability.

This is replay evidence, not an operational SOC claim. Real range arenas, external replication, dedicated verifier accounts, production signing custody, and live post-state/recovery verification remain blocked.
