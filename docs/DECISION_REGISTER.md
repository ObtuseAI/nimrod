## Decision record template

For a material decision add a dedicated ADR containing:

- status and decision owner;
- context and threat-model impact;
- options considered;
- decision and consequences;
- privacy/data/authority changes;
- migration and rollback;
- validation evidence;
- review date and superseded decisions.
